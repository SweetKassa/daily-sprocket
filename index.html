Daily Sprocket

Daily tech news on YouTube, told by Sprocket, a hand-drawn robot.

Daily Sprocket is a YouTube channel that covers the most important technology news every day in a fast, story-driven episode.

This site also hosts the information for Daily Sprocket Uploader, a private tool the channel owner uses to upload finished episodes to the channel through the YouTube Data API.
