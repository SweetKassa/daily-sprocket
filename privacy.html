Privacy Policy

Daily Sprocket Uploader · last updated 4 October 2026

Daily Sprocket Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Daily Sprocket YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to upload and manage videos on the owner's YouTube channel (YouTube Data API scopes). It uses this only to upload episodes, set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, and read back the upload status.

How data is stored

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machine or private server, and are never shared with third parties. The app does not collect, sell or share any personal data of viewers or other users.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Daily Sprocket Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
